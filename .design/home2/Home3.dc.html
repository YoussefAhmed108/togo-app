Catch-up — finite by design
Three blocks, one item each, and an explicit end: what your friends did, one pick for you, and one place you saved and never went to. No infinite scroll, no rails, nothing competing. Every block links out to the tab that owns it, so Home never duplicates a list.
The “saved but never visited” block is the one genuinely new idea here — you already store a visited flag and never use it. Low effort, high value.
